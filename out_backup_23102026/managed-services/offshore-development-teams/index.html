Frequently Asked Questions About Offshore Development

An offshore development team is a group of technology professionals based in another country who work on software development or IT projects for a business.

They collaborate with internal teams through defined roles, project management processes, communication channels, and shared delivery goals.

Teams can include software developers, QA engineers, DevOps specialists, designers, technical leads, and project coordinators.

Businesses may consider offshore teams when they need additional technical expertise, flexible capacity, or support for ongoing development projects.
