Cloud Migration Services help businesses move applications, data, and IT workloads from on-premises systems or existing cloud environments to a new cloud platform.

Timelines depend on the number of workloads, their dependencies, data volumes, and the migration approach chosen. A focused migration of a few applications may take weeks, while a large, complex estate can take several months. A readiness assessment gives you a realistic schedule with milestones.

Cloud migration moves whole workloads, including applications, infrastructure, and configurations, to the cloud. Data migration is one part of that effort: transferring databases and files with attention to integrity, security, and validation. A data migration can also happen on its own, for example between storage systems.

Downtime is reduced by assessing dependencies up front, migrating in planned phases, keeping data synchronized until cutover, testing before go-live, and preparing rollback options. Scheduling cutovers outside peak business hours also limits the impact on users.

Readiness depends on having a clear picture of your applications and dependencies, defined business goals, security and compliance requirements, and the skills to run workloads in the cloud. A cloud readiness assessment reviews these areas and highlights any gaps to close before you migrate.
