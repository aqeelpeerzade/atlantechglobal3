Intelligent Commerce is the use of AI, automation, and data to make e-commerce more personalised, responsive, and efficient. It can include AI-powered search, product recommendations, conversational commerce, personalisation, and automated commerce workflows.

AI-powered e-commerce uses machine learning, natural-language processing, and automation to improve how customers find, evaluate, and buy products, and how businesses run their online stores. Common examples include semantic product search, personalised recommendations, and AI shopping assistants.

AI can help customers find relevant products faster, show recommendations based on behaviour and product interactions, answer questions through conversational assistants, and automate repetitive support and workflow tasks. It can also surface patterns in customer and product data that guide better commerce decisions.

Conversational commerce lets customers discover products, ask questions, and get purchase assistance through AI-powered conversations, such as chat assistants on a website or app, instead of browsing and searching alone.

An AI shopping assistant is an automated tool that provides product guidance and purchase assistance. It can understand what a customer is looking for, suggest suitable products, answer common questions, and support the customer through the buying journey.

Yes. Intelligent Commerce solutions are typically connected to your existing e-commerce platform, product catalogue, and business systems through integrations and APIs, so you can add capabilities such as smarter search or recommendations without rebuilding your store.

E-commerce automation handles repetitive tasks, such as routine support and order workflows, using fixed rules. Intelligent Commerce adds AI on top, so the experience can understand customer intent, personalise what each shopper sees, and learn from data as well as automate processes.
